# Governing-intent Pies and Engram archaeology

Status: evidence for revision; not ratification

This pass tested `docs/governing-intent.md` against the strongest applicable
Pies and Engram prior art. The repositories are historical project doctrine and
working machinery, not external proof. Agreement supports transfer; conflict or
later correction constrains it.

## Verdict

The principal is directionally sound and mutually beneficial, but the original
candidate overstated three things:

1. it phrased mu, maybe and Bool as identities rather than responsibility and
   authority loci in a composed loop;
2. it promised total semantic losslessness where the stronger later doctrine is
   preservation-or-declared-loss; and
3. it described monadic recursion as achieved algebra rather than a governing
   admission obligation whose carriers and laws remain to be implemented.

The candidate has been revised, but remains unratified.

## Pies findings

- ADR-0179 directly maps `bool / maybe / mu` to law / agent / human and requires
  that mu or maybe never masquerade as Bool. It supports the triad while making
  the rung a provenance and authority type.
- ADR-0192 makes the bridge a loop: read descends from mu through maybe,
  evaluation returns Bool or mu, and presentation returns to human acceptance.
  Pantheon therefore cannot honestly be described as only Bool.
- ADR-0203 closes the deterministic rail: unknown constructs and unconsumed
  fields refuse rather than warn, default or disappear.
- ADR-0220 corrects the earlier lossless-projection claim: projections may lose
  information, but loss must be declared and traceable to source identity and
  Rosetta.
- ADR-0275 treats Rune form as maybe-tier work folded into Bool-shaped
  discipline, but explicitly keeps working content at maybe and Bool as an
  asymptotic structural limit. The mathematical vocabulary is not itself proof
  of Pantheon's concrete composition laws.
- `steer.machine.rune` preserves operator choices to proceed, refine, defer or
  refuse. Operator collapse is prerogative, not theorem promotion.
- ADR-0264 preserves held superposition: supported plural readings may remain
  live rather than being forced into one premature answer.

Pies also separates interaction, resolution and imprint. Pantheon should retain
proposal, disposition, execution and durable learning as distinct typed facts.
Its fictional world model is creative prior art, not formal governance evidence.

## Engram findings

- `Engram.Agent.Fold` is genuinely recursive and retains the child tree and real
  leaves. It prevents regrouping from laundering disagreement: mu is absorbing
  and convergence is the meet over the subtree.
- One shared well-founded fuel measure bounds the entire recursive fold. Budget
  exhaustion returns an explicit mu terminal rather than a partial result
  presented as complete.
- The fold's representative output is explicitly lossy. Safety comes from
  retaining full evidence and making uncertainty monotone, not from claiming
  that every summary is lossless.
- `Engram.Focus` makes center precise as an outermost-to-tip cofree containment
  spine. Scope, lens and dispatch are orthogonal; governing context folds down
  the same explicit spine.
- Grounding proposals park before mutation; an elected human action writes
  them. Agent work follows proposal, human go, bounded dispatch and explicit
  admit/discard transitions.
- Trust gates, grant envelopes and command catalogs fail closed. Scope,
  principal, capability clamp and pinned model are authority-bearing inputs;
  naming an effect does not mint executable authority.
- Stable subject identity plus content anchors preserve provenance across
  insertions and explicitly orphan deleted evidence instead of silently
  mis-pointing.
- Its maintained twin checks incremental projections against full re-derivation.
  It also records the crucial reflexive lesson that instruments can invent bugs;
  a test becomes evidence only after its failure behavior is demonstrated.

## Resulting governing invariants

The archaeology supports these transfers into Pantheon:

1. Mu, maybe and Bool identify epistemic and authority roles, not participant
   castes.
2. Human prerogative includes surprising redirection but not infallibility. The
   agent must challenge weakening, preserve dissent and surface consequences;
   the human retains accountable disposition.
3. Human disposition cannot manufacture proof, stakeholder consent or owner
   fact.
4. Required semantics, authority, provenance, dissent and acceptance conditions
   are preserved. Any other projection loss is explicit, bounded and traceable.
5. Recursive work returns a typed evidence envelope, never only a representative
   string.
6. Uncertainty and refusal are absorbing under regrouping; recursion consumes one
   shared finite budget.
7. The center is locus-relative and explicit. Scope, lens and dispatch do not
   leak into one another.
8. Held plurality is a legitimate state and must not be averaged away.
9. Candidate prose, machine-checked predicates, owner facts and human-ratified
   intent remain distinct.
10. Execution closes back into evidence return and human acceptance or reopening.
11. The Gray is the Maybe Machine: maybe made operational as a typed,
    evidence-returning inquiry loop. Bounded experiment and control challenge
    are child paths, not the whole machine. It cannot self-promote a claim into
    Bool, compile away mu, collapse held plurality or silently bypass current
    authority.
12. Grounding is continuous and risk is altitude-sensitive. Every abstraction
    retains a cheapest safe descent toward the nearest owner-observable fact;
    local bounded uncertainty is preferable to distant sweeping confidence.

## The Maybe Machine correction

The archaeology's first revision still over-indexed on refusal and preservation.
That posture can become epistemically self-sealing: if a control prevents the
experiment needed to test whether the control is valid, strict compliance alone
can preserve dogma indefinitely.

The later name correction exposes the deeper structure: The Gray is not a place
beside maybe. It is the Maybe Machine—the operational discipline that retains
uncertainty while routing the least-cost adequate inquiry. Experiment is one
possible child computation beside proof, observation, deliberation, human
return, held plurality and refusal. Each returns evidence monadically; none may
mint its own Bool admission.

Pies' held-superposition discipline, operator steering cycle, inductive
pattern-emergence doctrine and separation of structural invariants from tunable
parameters support a protected experimental domain. Engram's strongest warning
points the same direction from operations: measuring apparatus and target models
can be wrong, so deliberately driving an instrument red is part of obtaining
evidence. Neither prior art warrants uncontrolled effects or theorem promotion.

The transfer is therefore two-sided:

- experiments must be bounded, attributable, observable, affordable and closed
  back into evidence;
- each unresolved claim must state its altitude, assumptions, blast radius,
  nearest owner-observable fact and cheapest adequate grounding descent;
- controls must declare purpose, scope, evidence, ownership and a challenge or
  amendment path;
- an experiment may test a control in a separately isolated policy or machine,
  but may not silently cross an authority or safety floor still in force; and
- supported, refuted and unresolved experimental outcomes retain their actual
  epistemic tier until separately admitted.

This makes advancement possible without treating either novelty or control as
self-justifying.

## Remaining admission work

The revised candidate still requires:

- exact-byte review by the human sponsor and independently attributable affected
  stakeholders;
- an owner-issued immutable identity and predecessor lineage;
- a disposition under `pantheon-governing-intent-admission.machine.rune`;
- concrete Rune/Scry/Smith support for singular typed child invocation and return
  before the monadic recursion claim can become executable; and
- a qualified multi-terminal path check for accepted, refused and
  revision-required admission exits.

Until those exist, repository hashes prove candidate provenance only.
