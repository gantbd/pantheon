# Pantheon Maybe Machine owner audit

Date: 2026-11-20

Status: grounded owner recommendation; no selected owner contract, executable
parent machine, runtime instance, authority, acceptance or rollout claim

## Question

After recognizing that The Gray is the Maybe Machine, which existing Pantheon
owners can lawfully carry its semantics without turning composition convenience
into epistemic authority?

## Current owner facts

Rune owns authored activity-machine semantics and Scry owns canonical structural
admission. The machine dialect does not currently admit the singular typed child
invocation/return required by the parent Maybe Machine. The retained
`pantheon-center-out-delivery-v2` evidence shows the exact `(invoke …)` refusal;
deleting that obligation or simulating it in an untyped wrapper would weaken the
design.

Moira currently owns two relevant but narrower runtime families:

- durable sagas retain explicit steps, effects, receipts, attempts,
  compensation and uncertainty fences; and
- methodical deliberation retains exact plans, finite fuel, attributed reports,
  dissent, validator witnesses and a closed terminal `ResolutionV1` of
  `ProvenBool`, `AuthorizedChoice`, four-valued `Maybe`, or reasoned `Mu`.

Those facts make Moira the strongest existing candidate to host durable Maybe
Machine instances and child-effect history. They do **not** establish that
Moira currently owns a general uncertain claim, its altitude and grounding
descent, inquiry selection, recursive child invocation/return, held plurality,
or a separately governed Bool admission. In particular, Moira's current
`Maybe(MaybeValueV1)` is a terminal result of one deliberation, not the parent
inquiry loop.

The remaining components retain their existing bounded roles:

- Dendrite/Tellus resolve authored method, context, expertise and concern
  evidence but do not select or settle an inquiry;
- Vellum may own revisioned inquiry artifacts and change proposals but not
  epistemic disposition;
- Chronos may retain admitted work and links but is not claim truth or semantic
  frontier authority;
- Agora carries attributable conversation, not task or claim state;
- Oculus correlates events, logs, metrics and traces, which orient but do not
  prove owner facts or admission;
- Aegis decides exact authority and disclosure, not truth;
- Daimon executes bounded agent/model work, not inquiry selection or acceptance;
  and
- Pantheon presents and composes the owner contracts without becoming a second
  owner of their facts.

## Recommended ownership split

1. **Rune owns the Maybe Machine definition and preservation laws.** The
   canonical machine names admissible child kinds, exact input/return carriers,
   shared well-founded budget, uncertainty/refusal monotonicity and the
   separately governed exit families.
2. **Moira is the candidate durable runtime host, not yet the selected semantic
   owner.** A new versioned workflow family may retain `MaybeInquiryV1`, current
   state, selected child, history, budget and exact child receipts only after its
   public owner contract and recovery laws are designed and qualified.
3. **Inquiry selection is an explicit decision.** A named planning authority
   selects one adequate child or retains non-selection. Moira may enforce and
   record that decision but cannot infer it from model confidence, votes,
   telemetry, available capabilities or elapsed time.
4. **Child owners keep their facts.** Deterministic proof, owner observation,
   experiment, deliberation and human-return children issue their own typed
   evidence/refusal. The parent retains references and verified envelopes; it
   does not reinterpret child success.
5. **Bool and mu remain outside self-promotion.** A Bool candidate crosses a
   separately qualified proof/admission gate. Irreducible judgment returns to
   named humans or stakeholders. Held plurality and safe refusal remain distinct
   from both.
6. **Pantheon exposes parity, not ownership.** TUI and headless views project the
   same inquiry identity, altitude, state, selected child, evidence, budgets,
   dissent, refusal and next safe action. Attach/detach changes presentation
   lifetime only.

## Smallest preserving delivery order

1. Design and admit Rune's typed child invocation/return construct, including
   recursion/cycle bounds, canonical round-trip, Scry validation, Rust Smith
   compatibility/refusal and negative tests. The bounded owner request is
   retained in `design/requests/rune-typed-child-invocation.rosetta.md`.
2. Define the exact `MaybeInquiryV1`, child-request and child-evidence carriers
   without choosing a persistence owner by type name alone.
3. Review Moira as the durable host against recovery, replay, uncertainty,
   authority, budget and migration requirements; select it only with owner
   evidence or retain the owner gap.
4. Qualify one read-only deterministic owner-observation child. It must return
   bounded owner evidence or explicit unavailable/truncated/uncertain outcomes
   and perform no effect.
5. Compose the already-admitted bounded-experiment child without granting it
   selection, Bool admission or control-override authority.
6. Present the exact parent inquiry through headless and TUI paths, including
   detach/reattach and owner reopen, before claiming operational parity.

This order starts at the missing load-bearing semantic edge and radiates
outward. It does not implement a speculative Moira schema, duplicate the current
deliberation engine, or make the existing experiment leaf appear recursive.
