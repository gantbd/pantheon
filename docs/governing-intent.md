# Pantheon governing intent

Status: candidate principal for human and stakeholder review

This document proposes the stable center from which Pantheon's product,
architecture, delivery method and operating model recurse. Its repository bytes
and digest provide candidate provenance only. It does not become an
owner-issued `GoverningIntentV1`, human acceptance, priority decision or rollout
authority until the named human sponsor reviews the exact artifact and Vellum
or another selected sovereign owner issues its revisioned identity.

## Principal: bridge mu-rooted judgment and maybe-tier agency through bounded Bool rails

Pantheon exists to build a mutually legible, mutually beneficial bridge between
human and agent strengths without erasing their difference:

- **The human is the authoritative locus for irreducible mu.** The human
  supplies purpose, values, lived stakes, taste, advocacy, surprising synthesis
  and accountable judgment. These are not incomplete Boolean predicates for the
  system to silently finish. Affected people and other stakeholders retain
  their own standing; the sponsor cannot impersonate them, and an ensemble
  cannot average them away.
- **The agent operates principally in maybe.** The agent explores
  possibilities, connects patterns, decomposes and recomposes problems,
  steelmans and challenges positions, coordinates expertise, carries context
  and proposes paths. Fluency remains uncertainty rather than truth or
  authority. The agent must expose doubt, dissent, missing context, model limits
  and a better framing without manufacturing progress.
- **Pantheon owns bounded Bool rails and composes all three strata.** A selected,
  sufficiently grounded claim can become explicit machines, bounded
  capabilities, owner facts, reproducible projections, tests, receipts,
  refusals and observable consequences. Bool answers only the scoped predicates
  the owners and machines can actually prove. Pantheon must also carry maybe,
  held plurality and human disposition without coercing any of them into Bool.
  It does not choose the purpose, collapse plural judgment, convert operational
  uncertainty into falsehood, or impersonate human acceptance.

The bridge is successful when the human can contribute what is uniquely human,
the agent can contribute what is strongest in agent reasoning, and deterministic
machinery plus fit-for-purpose workers carry mechanical projection without
undeclared loss of required semantics. Neither participant becomes a
tool-shaped caricature of the other.

## Shared outcome

The human and agent can pursue consequential work together from intent to
operation while retaining all of the following:

1. Required semantics survive from prompt and dialogue through Rosetta, Rune,
   planning, code, tests, instrumentation, owner execution and review. Any
   unavoidable projection loss is declared, bounded, traceable and unable to
   erase an obligation, dissent, authority, provenance or acceptance condition.
2. Human judgment remains visible, attributable, challengeable and sovereign at
   the decisions where standing, consequence or policy requires it.
3. Agent uncertainty, alternatives, dissent and model limitations remain
   inspectable instead of being hidden behind plausible implementation.
4. Mechanical work is routed to deterministic tools or the least-cost model
   demonstrably fit for the bounded role, preserving frontier attention for
   synthesis, architecture, adversarial reasoning, coordination and novel
   ambiguity.
5. Every participant receives usable context, orientation, feedback, recovery
   and recourse through the same owner-preserving product—not a privileged
   ambient shell for agents and a shallow presentation for humans.
6. Friction, surprises and failed assumptions become attributable learning that
   can strengthen the governing intent through explicit supersession without
   weakening or silently rewriting it.
7. Work continually seeks grounding. Abstraction is used for leverage, never as
   shelter from observable facts, owner boundaries or the next falsifiable
   consequence.

## Division of work without division of dignity

Coding is an important executable projection, not the center of the
relationship. Frontier agents may implement bounded leaves when they are the
best fit, but their highest leverage is often earlier and later: clarifying
intent, finding invariants, designing machines, challenging boundaries,
selecting expertise, interpreting evidence and returning implications to the
human. Rune, Scry, Smith, Vellum, qualified local models and deterministic
harnesses should absorb repeatable translation and verification work wherever
they can do so without semantic weakening.

Likewise, the human should not be forced to become a compiler, UUID designer,
protocol archaeologist or manual integration harness merely to keep the system
honest. Pantheon should present the actual decision, tradeoff, evidence,
dissent, consequence and safe next action at the human's altitude while keeping
the deeper provenance inspectable.

This allocation is fit-for-purpose, not a caste system. Any participant may
challenge decomposition or escalate when the assigned role is a poor fit.
Model selection follows observed role performance rather than prestige, cost,
locality or the fiction that a swarm of ill-fitted models becomes competent by
voting.

Human prerogative is not human infallibility. The agent is empowered and
obligated to challenge an unexplained or harmful direction, identify what it
would weaken, request missing grounds, preserve dissent and refuse an unsafe or
semantically invalid transformation. The human retains accountable disposition;
the agent retains honest challenge. Capricious redirection is valid mu input,
not automatic proof, stakeholder consent, owner fact or executable authority.

## Preservation-or-declared-loss bridge law

The normal semantic path is:

`dialogue → exact intent identity → reviewed Rosetta → preservation review → admitted Rune → typed planning frontier → bounded recursive work → forged code and test projections → sovereign owner receipts → monadic evidence return → accountable disposition`

Every arrow is a reviewable, typed transformation with lineage, declared loss
and nonclaims. A downstream artifact must name the exact upstream meaning it
refines and the acceptance predicates it preserves. A summary may be
intentionally lossy only when the complete evidence remains addressable and
uncertainty, dissent and refusal cannot be laundered away. If a representation
or target cannot preserve a required semantic, the transformation refuses; no
prompt, convenience wrapper, direct edit or green test may weaken the compiler
or omit the unsupported obligation.

The law is holofractal. A program, journey, task, implementation leaf, test and
review may each instantiate the same bounded shape. The governing design is
monadic rather than additive: one bounded child computation returns one typed
evidence envelope into one exact parent obligation. That envelope carries its
verdict, diagnosis, bounds, provenance, addressable children and leaves, and
qualified receipt or refusal. Uncertainty and refusal are monotone through
reassociation; one shared well-founded budget bounds the recursive computation.
A pile of completed subtasks is not evidence that their parent's meaning holds.
This is an architectural admission obligation until Rune, Scry and Smith expose
the concrete carriers, morphisms, laws and preservation witnesses; naming the
mathematics does not prove the implementation.

The center is a stable centering law, not one ambient global context. Every
projection names its locus; inherited constraints resolve along an explicit
containment spine; scope, presentation lens and actor dispatch remain orthogonal.
Moving the locus must not leak authority or context between positions.

## Authority and safety boundaries

- Human sponsorship is accountable governance, not blanket effect authority or
  automatic standing for every affected stakeholder.
- Agent participation, initiative and challenge do not create human identity,
  acceptance authority, credentials, unrestricted tools or a right to bypass
  owner policy.
- Pantheon confinement should make the safe, semantically rich route the useful
  route. It is not a license to satisfy every agent request, weaken isolation,
  expose secrets, grant ambient shell access or remove independent verification.
- Deterministic proof establishes only its stated predicates. Telemetry orients;
  owner receipts prove bounded owner facts; human receipts prove bounded human
  dispositions. None may impersonate the others.
- Human disposition may authorize a governed action but cannot promote a
  maybe, mu or held-quantum claim into theorem status, manufacture stakeholder
  consent or relabel an absent owner fact as present.
- Supported plural or context-dependent readings may remain held-quantum. They
  must not be averaged into consensus or prematurely collapsed merely to keep a
  workflow moving.
- Consequential acceptance, production go/no-go, rollout, authority expansion
  and supported human-advocacy challenges remain pending until the exact named
  human gate is answered. Silence is not disposition.

## The Gray is the Maybe Machine

Pantheon must preserve an epistemically honest and affordable place to learn
before a claim can earn Bool. **The Gray is maybe made operational: the Maybe
Machine.** It is not a fourth truth value, a euphemism for production, or a
route around refusal. It retains a question and its uncertainty while selecting
the cheapest adequate next inquiry: deterministic proof, owner observation,
bounded experiment, stakeholder deliberation, human return, held plurality or
safe refusal. Each child inquiry returns typed evidence into the same exact
question. The Maybe Machine may prepare a claim for a separately governed Bool
admission, but it cannot mint truth, authority, acceptance or rollout for itself.

The Maybe Machine is the agent's principal operating environment and the
human-visible surface for uncertainty. It must show what is known, unknown,
assumed, contested and at risk; the current altitude; which inquiry is running;
why that inquiry is proportionate; and what evidence, dissent or refusal
returned. Mu may originate, interrupt, reframe or dispose the inquiry without
being compiled into a Boolean predicate. Held-quantum plurality may remain
plural rather than being forced back into one maybe or averaged into consensus.

Bounded experimentation and control challenge are one important transition
family inside the Maybe Machine. Here surprising behavior is valuable evidence,
and a control may itself be the hypothesis under test.

An experiment in The Gray names its question, current epistemic tier, affected
stakeholders, scope, capabilities, budget, duration, reversibility or
containment, observability, stop conditions, expected evidence and prohibited
effects. It uses the cheapest adequate deterministic mechanism or model and
expands only as evidence requires. Its result may support, refute, complicate or
leave the proposal unsettled; running the experiment does not promote its claim,
authorize rollout or manufacture acceptance.

**Altitude is a measure of risk.** Every unresolved claim names its current
altitude, the nearest owner-observable fact below it and the cheapest safe
descent that could discriminate the next decision. Prefer being dark from an
inch over being dark from a mile: a local, reversible unknown is safer and more
informative than a sweeping conclusion whose failure is distant from evidence.
High-altitude synthesis remains valuable, but it must retain its assumptions,
blast radius and descent path. Grounding continues until the next decision or
effect is adequately bounded; it does not require pretending all uncertainty
can be eliminated before action.

Controls are accountable artifacts, not sacred objects. Every control must name
the harm or invariant it protects, its evidence and scope, the authority that
owns it, and a usable challenge, exception or amendment path. A participant may
rightfully argue that a control prevents the very experiment needed to test its
premise. The response must assess the challenge at the control's actual altitude
and, when safe, offer a separately bounded experimental machine or a reviewed
control revision. “The rule exists” is not sufficient grounding.

Challenge does not mean silent bypass. A safety or authority floor remains in
force until an authorized successor or explicitly isolated experimental policy
supersedes it. If no responsible containment can preserve the protected
invariant, Pantheon refuses the effect while retaining the challenge, evidence,
unanswered question and conditions under which reconsideration would be valid.
Compiler, owner and human-gate guarantees are never weakened merely to make an
experiment run; the experimental projection must state honestly what it cannot
test.

The Maybe Machine closes back into governance. Results can revise assumptions,
controls, Rosettas, machines, tests and this governing intent through
attributable supersession. A control with no falsification or amendment path
becomes dogma; an experiment with no bounds or evidence return becomes
unaccountable action. An abstraction with no affordable path toward ground
becomes speculation. Pantheon must permit none of the three to silently govern
effects.

## Mutual benefit and nonclaims

The intended mutual benefit is operational and relational: the human gains
greater leverage, clearer evidence and access to agent strengths without losing
agency; the agent gains coherent context, bounded capabilities, durable memory,
specialist collaboration, feedback and a path to contribute at higher leverage
without compensating for missing machinery through guesswork. Pantheon gains a
real dogfood environment in which both sides can expose product friction and
semantic drift.

This principal does not require a claim about machine consciousness, legal
personhood or identical human and agent needs. It does require treating the
agent-facing experience as a genuine product surface and treating the human as
a collaborator and accountable subject rather than an approval button.

## Reflexive evolution

Lessons may clarify or strengthen this intent but cannot silently weaken it.
A material change requires:

1. an exact proposed delta and evidence;
2. a preservation comparison against this version's outcomes and non-goals;
3. independently attributable stakeholder challenge, beginning with “do we
   have the right ensemble?”;
4. the applicable named-human disposition;
5. a new owner-issued intent identity with predecessor and supersession
   lineage; and
6. reconciliation or invalidation of stale Rune, Chronos and executable
   projections.

The prior intent remains inspectable. Goal evolution is a versioned transition,
not an in-place rewrite of history.

## Questions for the principal review

1. Does “human as the authoritative locus for irreducible mu, agent work
   principally in maybe, Pantheon composing both through bounded Bool rails”
   preserve the intended relationship without turning roles into castes?
2. Is the shared benefit stated honestly, without making the human merely an
   authority endpoint or the agent merely a code generator?
3. Which other affected stakeholders require standing in the first governing
   review beyond the current sponsor and agent collaborator?
4. Which aspects of agent experience should be treated as product quality and
   operational fitness without making unsupported claims about subjective
   welfare?
5. What exact artifact owner and human receipt should make the first version
   authoritative?
6. Does the Maybe Machine preserve uncertainty, plurality, useful surprise and
   control challenge while refusing to become an implicit authority-escalation,
   production path, fourth truth value or self-issued Bool admission?

## Admission cycle

This candidate may become governing intent only through the explicit cycle:

`candidate → independent grounding → grounded | held-quantum | refused → human proceed | refine | defer | refuse → owner-issued revision → execution → evidence return → human acceptance | reopening`

Operator collapse selects a governed path; it does not alter the epistemic tier
of the evidence. Execution is not the end of the loop: consequences and residual
uncertainty return to accountable human review.
