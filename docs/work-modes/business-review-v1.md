# BusinessReviewV1

Status: proposed initial Work-Mode Registry definition

Semantic owner: the organization-designated business-review owner

Accountable human: named Executive Sponsor

## Purpose

Run a monthly operating review or quarterly strategic review as an evidence-
bound decision instrument rather than a status-performance ritual.

## Required inputs

- period, scope, evidence cutoff and completeness;
- objectives, expected outcomes and current assumptions;
- operating, financial, customer/stakeholder and risk evidence;
- portfolio commitments and dependencies;
- ensemble reports, dissent and human-gate requests.

## Sections

1. Executive cover and material-change narrative.
2. Objectives and expected-versus-observed outcomes.
3. Operating, financial, delivery, quality and reliability posture.
4. Stakeholder and human-impact review.
5. Risks, opportunities, uncertainty and missing evidence.
6. Decision agenda with exact alternatives and consequences.
7. Next-period outcome commitments.
8. Evidence, metric-definition, dissent and receipt appendices.

Monthly cadence emphasizes exceptions and near-term intervention. Quarterly
cadence additionally revisits strategy, investment, scenarios and continuation.

## Outcomes and transitions

`draft -> evidence_ready -> ensemble_review -> sponsor_decision -> published -> superseded`

Each decision item records go, no-go, revise or defer. A human-advocate finding
may require a separate named-human gate. Publication does not execute peer
effects or prove an outcome.

## Nonclaims

The report does not own source metrics, tasks, budgets, authority, peer state or
final production rollout. A green summary cannot erase dissent, uncertainty or
missing populations.
