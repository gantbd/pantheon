# ProjectProposalV1

Status: proposed initial Work-Mode Registry definition

Semantic owner: the organization-designated proposal owner

Accountable human: named Executive Sponsor

## Purpose

Turn a problem or opportunity into an explicitly sponsored, evidence-bound and
stage-gated investment without replaying initial sponsorship as later approval.

## Required inputs

- problem/opportunity evidence and affected stakeholders;
- intended outcomes, baseline, success/failure/stop criteria;
- status quo, minimum intervention, recommendation and alternatives;
- scope/non-goals, architecture, dependencies and owner boundaries;
- cost range, capacity, opportunity cost and funding stages;
- risk, human impact, delivery, validation, rollback and exit evidence.

## Stages

1. Sponsor intent.
2. Discovery authorization.
3. Proposal and funding decision.
4. Build authorization.
5. Pilot authorization.
6. Named-human final rollout go/no-go.
7. Benefits-realization review.
8. Continue, revise, stop or close.

Every stage pins the proposal revision, evidence, alternatives, dissent,
exposure/funding bounds, accountable principal, Aegis decision where required,
response and next gate. A changed material input invalidates the prior response.

## Human advocacy

The ensemble includes a human-advocate lens selected for the affected context.
An admitted supported and non-frivolous gate request pauses the exact stage for
named-human adjudication. The advocate gains no effect authority; the sponsor or
work owner cannot bypass presentation through automatic disposition.

## Nonclaims

Sponsorship is not funding receipt, task admission, technical acceptance,
authority, deployment, final rollout approval or proof of realized benefit.
