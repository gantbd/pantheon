# Fractal capability, boundary and prior-art review

This is a required companion to Pantheon's delivery operating contract. It
turns introspection into a bounded learning loop rather than an occasional
architecture exercise or an excuse to centralize peer semantics.

## Epistemic center

For every material review, find the smallest load-bearing claim whose truth
would make the surrounding design cohere. State that center plainly, then try
to break it.

- Drive a decidable claim down to Boolean evidence: exact preconditions,
  authoritative observations, falsifiable counterexamples and a bounded
  verifier. `true` is scoped proof, never confidence, consensus or absence of a
  detected failure. `false` is useful evidence and changes the next action.
- `maybe` is a transient work state, not a flattering verdict. Gather evidence
  until it resolves to Boolean truth/falsity, or show why the question is
  genuinely plural, value-laden, under-specified or undecidable in the admitted
  frame and raise it to explicit mu/human judgment.
- Preserve operational unknown and outcome-unknown as owner facts. Do not call
  transport ambiguity mu, and do not turn human judgment into a Boolean test.
- Crystallize only around a center that survives adversarial examples,
  independent evidence and boundary review. Ensemble agreement can suggest the
  center; it cannot prove it.
- If no honest center is yet visible, record the competing candidates, missing
  evidence and next discriminating observation. Clarity includes refusing a
  premature synthesis.

The center is an invariant or decision fulcrum, not a universal abstraction.
Different sovereign owners may remain irreducibly different while composing
around one shared truth such as identity preservation, no invented authority,
or uncertainty that cannot be silently retried.

## Review geometry

Apply the same ownership, evidence, user-advocacy and operational questions at
each material scale.

| Direction | Examine | Required question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breadth | journeys, stakeholders, peers, surfaces, signals and environments | What legitimate user or operator need is absent, duplicated or undiscoverable? |
| Depth | presentation, behavior facade, client, protocol, owner, persistence and effects | Where does meaning, authority, uncertainty or identity leak across a layer? |
| Up | objective, outcome, portfolio, sponsor and public/user consequence | Does local success still advance the governed journey and accountable outcome? |
| Down | command, field, state transition, receipt, failure mode and measurement | Can the claim be traced to exact owner evidence and adversarial qualification? |
| In | cohesion, coupling, duplication, performance, accessibility and cognitive load | Is the implementation honest, economical, reusable and comfortable to operate? |
| Out | users, adjacent systems, standards, ecosystem precedent, privacy and redress | What external effect, dependency, expectation or affected person is missing? |
| Across time | admission, operation, restart, migration, retirement and review | Does meaning survive change without replaying stale authority or erasing residue? |

Stop descending when the next level is immaterial to the decision, repeats an
already-qualified law, or lacks evidence that would change action. Record that
stopping reason. Reopen when risk, observed failure, a new consumer or a changed
standard supplies new evidence.

## Prior-art transfer protocol

Engram and Pies are standing prior-art inputs alongside peer contracts,
standards, domain theory and live dogfood. They are evidence, not authorities.
For every candidate transfer record:

1. the exact source artifact and observed behavior;
2. whether the value is direct reuse, owner-preserving adaptation,
   presentation inspiration, or a warning to reject;
3. the Pantheon journey and user problem it improves;
4. the sovereign semantic owner and the Pantheon projection/router boundary;
5. invariants, limits, privacy and uncertainty behavior that must survive;
6. the smallest qualified slice, TUI/headless evidence and rollback boundary;
7. the accepted Chronos work, accountable owner and next review trigger; and
8. the Vellum artifact, Dendrite grounding and Moira deliberation/disposition
   that preserve why the transfer was or was not made.

Similarity of names or shapes is not enough. A shared abstraction is earned
only when independent consumers have identical semantics and failure laws.

## Rune execution discipline

Rune is the preferred executable specification and grounded-method substrate
for this review, not a source of decorative diagrams. Reuse the shared Rune
machines before writing a Pantheon-specific lifecycle:

- `audit-cycle.machine.rune` governs recurring scan, comparison, triage,
  investigation, close/disclose and durable recording;
- `gap-analysis.machine.rune` governs bounded breadth/depth gap review;
- `prior-art-search.machine.rune` governs multi-angle precedent discovery;
- `ensemble-generator.machine.rune` makes perspective selection and ensemble
  assembly explicit before execution;
- `instrumented-bdd.machine.rune` separates behavior, pathway evidence,
  adversarial validation and finding disposition; and
- `user-acceptance-driven-development.machine.rune` retains the real human
  review stage before completion.

Use Rune's tool chain at the boundary it actually proves. `scry` establishes
single-file structural invariants; `machine_path_check` proves selected graph
and mandatory-stage properties; `smith` supplies domain-language and
loss-checked presentation projections; `rune_loader`, reference/fold/lattice
checks and Dendrite supply bounded corpus resolution and expertise access.
None of those tools proves that prose is true, performs a real adversarial
attempt, records a human acceptance, executes the workflow, or owns a product
instance. Those claims require live owner evidence and named-human action.

The Pantheon-specific boundary graph is
[`../spec/delivery-governance.flow.rune`](../spec/delivery-governance.flow.rune).
Its source is canonical; generated Mermaid, PlantUML, RDF and SHACL are
disposable projections. The graph deliberately retains the unselected
sovereign review-definition owner as a visible gap.

### Directionally correct gaps

Treat a sound abstraction with incomplete tooling differently from an invalid
abstraction. Preserve its proven boundary, disclose the missing proof or
interaction, and create owner-bound remediation rather than replacing it with
shell glue or an untyped host convention. Current Rune gaps material to this
goal are:

1. the executable validator is the grammar authority, but a complete current
   standalone grammar projection is not maintained;
2. mandatory graph stages are checkable, but guards, preconditions and prose
   outcomes are not semantically proven;
3. context-to-SME folding is qualified, but the bounded gap-analysis machine
   does not itself author the relevant context tags;
4. Rune describes methods and flows but is not their runtime instance owner;
5. generated RDF/SHACL validates a generic flow vocabulary, not Pantheon's
   domain-specific authority or ownership semantics; and
6. Pantheon has no first-class Rune/Dendrite workbench yet for validation,
   language view, references, grounding, projections and located failures;
7. Dendrite's ordinary local start fails closed when registered client secrets
   are absent, but the startup error does not orient a user to credential setup
   or the documented isolated disabled-auth development route; and
8. several relevant groundings, including axiomatic reduction and formal
   methods, remain legacy whole-role content without ambient/activated/reference
   surfaces. Dendrite refuses the unsupported depth honestly and `describe`
   still exposes exact content, but the experience breaks the preferred
   bounded-surface loop.

These are legitimate Rune/Pantheon integration backlog candidates. They do not
justify copying Rune's shared machines into Pantheon or claiming structural
validation is operational completion.

## Standards and practice anchors

- [OpenTelemetry signals](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/) keep
  traces, metrics, logs, baggage, events and profiles distinguishable; events
  and profiles have different maturity and must not be overclaimed.
- [OpenTelemetry semantic conventions](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/specs/semconv/)
  provide names, units and resource conventions where they fit. Pantheon keeps
  owner-specific semantics and records convention/version maturity.
- [W3C Trace Context](https://www.w3.org/TR/trace-context/) is precedent for
  bounded cross-process correlation and explicitly forbids sensitive data in
  trace identifiers/state.
- [CloudEvents](https://github.com/cloudevents/spec) is precedent for portable
  event context, not authorization, integrity, delivery or domain semantics.
- [OpenLineage](https://github.com/OpenLineage/OpenLineage/blob/main/website/docs/spec/object-model.md)
  is useful precedent for separating design-time metadata, runtime runs and
  datasets. Pantheon must not invent UUID contracts for nontechnical owners or
  coerce all work into its object model.
- [Google SRE monitoring practice](https://sre.google/sre-book/monitoring-distributed-systems/)
  separates symptoms from causes, black-box from white-box evidence, and
  actionable paging from lower-urgency tickets. Golden signals are a baseline,
  not a substitute for journey or user-outcome evidence.
- [SLO burn-rate alerting](https://sre.google/workbook/alerting-on-slos/) is
  precedent for urgency based on budget consumption and time-to-act; thresholds
  remain owner-configured and evidence-backed.
- [NIST AI RMF](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf) supports
  explicit human roles, responsibilities and governance. Pantheon retains its
  stronger named-human gates for consequential rollout and contestable harm.

## Initial gaps and candidate transfers

1. Build a versioned instrumentation catalogue: signal type, source, schema,
   unit/population, sampling, retention, cardinality, privacy class, tier,
   owner and headless/TUI route.
2. Add query/follow and pivots among owner events, logs, metrics, traces,
   profiles, alerts and receipts without copying them into Hermes.
3. Propagate bounded content-safe trace context across peer calls and retain
   links to exact owner operations; define trust-boundary restart behavior.
4. Add user-visible SLO/error-budget and alert views with configurable quiet
   periods, escalation and ticket/page distinctions.
5. Convert useful investigation probes into bounded operation spans; delete
   noisy step logs only after equivalent evidence is qualified.
6. Feed recurring user-friction, SRE and pattern findings into Moira for
   attributable disposition and Chronos for accepted work.
7. Make Dendrite the grounded corpus for standards, precedent, known failure
   patterns and transfer decisions; it is not the live telemetry store.
8. Evaluate replacing Pantheon's bespoke bounded instrumentation vectors with
   Keystone's already adopted `keystone-observe::CaptureBuffer`. Promote only
   if overload, ordering, disabled-capture and privacy semantics match exactly.
9. Consider a small shared trace-context/envelope mechanism in Keystone only
   after at least two independent peer protocols demonstrate identical bounds,
   trust and propagation laws.

## Placement decisions and refusals

- Hermes remains the ordered domain-event ledger: append, replay, gap,
  compaction and independent cursors. It does not become the log/metric/trace
  backend, alert manager, audit authority or semantic event registry.
- Keystone may own small qualified mechanisms and laws. It must not own
  Pantheon tabs, review policy, cross-peer routing, domain event schemas,
  retry/reconciliation, stakeholder findings or human decisions.
- Pantheon owns composition, navigation, correlation presentation and routing.
  It does not become the authoritative store for peer facts.
- Moira owns bounded workflow/deliberation and finding disposition, not raw
  telemetry. Dendrite owns grounded expertise projections, not operational
  truth. Vellum owns review artifacts, not approval. Chronos owns admitted work,
  not the meaning of every signal that motivated it.
- Refuse a universal `Component`, event, telemetry or status protocol. Share
  only envelopes/mechanisms whose semantics and limits have actually converged.

## Tellus boundary decision

Tellus is the direct Pantheon component for grounded methods, context and
provenance. Dendrite owns the loaded corpus and bounded query/observation
semantics; Tellus owns their human and typed-client presentation; Pantheon
composes that component. MCP is one outward adapter for compatible agent
harnesses, not the canonical internal contract and not a second component
identity. ACP, CLI, SDK or future Claude/Codex/Gemini/Muse adapters may project
the same owner-selected contracts without becoming alternate sources of truth.

This naming is fractal: `tellus` is used in routes, capabilities, profiles,
headless peer identity, behavior actions, source receipts, fixtures and tests.
The word `grounding` remains only where it names a Rune form, evidence act,
digest or execution binding. No public/private or old/new naming split is
introduced.
